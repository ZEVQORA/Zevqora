WHY ZEVQORA

Your AI bill is a system problem, not a model picker problem.

Cost leaks hide across repeated work, oversized models, retries, context, routing, unnecessary LLM calls, and architecture. ZEVQORA follows the full execution path instead of stopping at a provider bill.

Repeated work
The same reasoning, summaries, or extraction steps paid for again and again.
Oversized models
Frontier-model cost on bounded tasks that can safely use a lighter path.
Unnecessary calls
LLM steps where deterministic logic, reuse, caching, or lookup is enough.
Retries + routing
Fallback chains and retry storms that quietly multiply unit cost.
Context weight
Prompts and histories that grew without a cost/quality review.
Architecture
System-level choices that make expensive execution the default.
