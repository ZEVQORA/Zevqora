### Exact generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: an extraordinary cinematic art-direction key visual for the ZEVQORA AI engineering website, wide 16:9 landscape composition. Primary request: a monumental single stream of computational signal, built from thousands of exquisitely fine, densely laminated electric-cobalt optical ribbons. A complex, three-dimensional, open S-shaped surge crosses the frame from lower left foreground to upper right background, separating into precise individual filament sheets on one end and resolving into one incredibly smooth, coherent flow on the other. Macro-scale depth and perspective, extraordinary fine detail, physically believable blue translucent polymer and optical fiber, satin cobalt faces with tiny bright icy-blue edge highlights, strong sculptural silhouette, dramatic controlled studio lighting in a completely ink-black environment. An advanced motion-design studio's technical AI campaign, tactile and refined, intense contrast, premium CGI with realistic optical properties. Not fabric or liquid paint: engineered computational material with rigorous repeating microstructure. The object is HUGE, occupies the right two-thirds, enters from outside the bottom edge, elegant unexpected perspective. Leave the far left 25% and upper left dark and quiet so real HTML typography can sit over it. The background is #080A0F ink, the only accent hue is intense cobalt/soft electric blue, no purple rainbow. Do not typeset anything. No words, no letters, no logos, no UI, no watermark. No glowing globe, no floating ball, no circle or ring, no portal or black hole, no building or architecture, no gold or silver jewelry, no human, no robot, no smoke, no random stars. This must read as a photographed volumetric computational flow, not a generic generative-AI wallpaper.
